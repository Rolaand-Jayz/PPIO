STATE OF THE PROJECT WHAT IS ESTABLISHED

A strong story.
A game to prove.

Playtime Protocol: Innocence Lost has a coherent narrative foundation, a named four-character party, a world structure, and a developing combat direction. It is still in preproduction.

The website presents the intended game, not a claim that the game already exists in playable form. Characters, proportions, rooms, enemy depictions and effects in the illustrations are concept explorations. Detailed production specifications will follow tested decisions.

THE NEXT MILESTONE VERTICAL SLICE DESIGN

One slice.
Every hard question.

A meaningful prototype must validate interconnected systems. A beautiful scene or a combat-only demo would not be enough.

01 / EXPLORATION

A believable miniature world

Construct a physically coherent piece of domestic geography and test the four bodies in it: wheels, low flight, humanoid movement, and exoskeleton clearance.

  • Scale and navigation readability
  • Camera and collision reliability
  • Interesting traversal for every hero
02 / ENCOUNTERS

Combat inside the environment

Prototype the positional, turn-based design with actual toy-scale obstacles and understandable enemy behavior.

  • Action and turn economy
  • Spatial clarity and pacing
  • Environmental interaction and counterplay
03 / PARTY STRUCTURE

Solo, pair, and four together

Test whether the mechanics remain viable with different party configurations instead of relying on the full roster to hide weaknesses.

  • Distinct character roles
  • Two-character cooperation
  • Independent decisions in solo play
04 / NARRATIVE

Story told through action

Demonstrate a grounded emotional beat that depends on character decision-making and the toys’ limited viewpoint.

  • CJ perspective rule
  • Earned character behavior
  • Readable cinematic transitions

QUALITY BAR THE ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The prototype must
earn its confidence.

01

It must be playable.

A real sequence with reliable controls, battles, transitions, and feedback—not a trailer substitute.

02

It must be readable.

Targets, positions, environmental affordances, threats, and action consequences must be understandable.

03

It must scale.

Solo, paired, and full-party encounters must function without fundamentally different combat rules.

04

It must feel like toys.

Materials, movement, geometry and abilities must reinforce toy identity rather than arbitrary fantasy character behavior.

05

It must support the story.

Gameplay choices and the emotional beat must reinforce one another instead of competing for attention.

06

It must survive revision.

Design findings should drive decisions before content production commits to expensive assumptions.

DESIGN INTEGRITY THE RULES THAT MATTER

Preserve the heart.
Question the rest.

01

The home must always feel worth protecting.

Fear and suspense must not erase the household’s identity, ordinary warmth or recognizable geometry.

02

CJ is visible only from the toys’ perspective.

Human-story events cannot rely on convenient omniscient cutaways that break the established observation rule.

03

The alley choice belongs to the toys.

The grandparents' heirlooms demonstrate experience, but do not instruct the protagonists to turn around.

04

Characters prove growth by doing something different.

Gameplay, blocking, and choices matter more than explanatory speeches or a simple change in lighting.

05

No claim of production completion without validation.

Concept artwork is not a gameplay screenshot. A proposed system is not a shipped feature.

QUESTIONS CURRENT ANSWERS

What we know.
What comes next.

Is the game playable yet?

Not as a validated, publicly available game. This site is a design and narrative showcase. The vertical slice is the next crucial development milestone.

Is there a release date or wishlist page?

No date, store listing, price, or release platform has been confirmed. The site will not provide a fake wishlist or trailer link.

Are the characters’ visuals final?

No. The artwork illustrates the intended tone, cast roles and scale but is not an approved final production asset set.

Is the combat turn-based?

The leading concept is a positional turn-based approach within the 3D world, paired with real-time exploration. Final rules and feel depend on vertical-slice testing.

Is the Unknown already designed?

Its thematic narrative function is established. Its actual form, abilities, origins, enemy types and world effects remain undecided.

Where can I read the full plot?

The story page includes the established narrative arc and an opt-in spoiler disclosure for the intended ending.
