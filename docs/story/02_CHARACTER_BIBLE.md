# Character bible — playable toy party

**Shared constraint:** Every hero is visibly an owned, manufactured toy, not a miniaturized flesh-and-blood person. Presentation, joints, scratches, scale and silhouette matter; mechanical abilities must remain readable from the gameplay camera.

## Jayze
Anime action-hero *archetype*, not a replica of a protected character. Expressive human-shaped action figure; melee **weapon** and offensive techniques. Balanced speed and medium-high damage as early intentions, not final combat numbers. Starts overconfident; learns responsibility, humility and empathy. Relationship with Jonnie is tested by his failure to understand helplessness. On return, he acknowledges others and can let Kip lead. Rig: humanoid articulation with authored weapon attachments.

## Kip
Small Hot Wheels-sized **weaponized race car**. Not humanoid, no arms/legs grafted onto a car body. Vehicle physics and mechanical tactics; body/steering/wheels/bumper/mounted weapon; fast movement and light-to-medium damage. His independence must be shown through action, especially when Rallo depends on him or the usual route is unavailable. Wheel motion and chassis response are key animation contracts.

## Rallo
Small, muscular gargoyle-like comic-book monster toy; short to the ground, with limited low flight/hover. Melee without a separate weapon, adaptable jack-of-all-trades abilities. Medium-fast, medium damage as provisional classification. Fear, disorientation and choosing courage are crucial, including a period when separated from Kip. Do not treat wings as permission for limitless flight; animated clearances and terrain rules apply.

## Captain Crusher / “Jonnie”
Female, battle-worn soldier action figure in heavy mech-like exoskeleton. A garage-sale pickup from a previous home with an only child of divorced parents. Heavy melee, slower movement, defensive/healing support. She starts with a go-it-alone worldview; early separation/pursuit explores its cost. The team must **earn** her trust rather than win it with one speech. The later reveal of “Jonnie” signifies closeness. Keep exoskeleton mechanics and repairs physically plausible.

## Relationships and design constraints
- Jayze/Jonnie: Jayze learns empathy after Jonnie resents abandonment; Jonnie learns trust and forgiveness.
- Kip/Rallo: Kip's independence and Rallo's courage are interconnected but not interchangeable.
- Respect distinct voices, physical capabilities, toy dimensions and lived histories.
- No exact costume details, scale, final move names, rig proportions, model art, or final weapon designs are approved merely by this bible.
- All prototypes are labeled `debug_only` or `concept_only` until approved.
