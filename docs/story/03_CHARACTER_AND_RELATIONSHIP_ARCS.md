## Tracking template
For each accepted scene: scene ID, toy vantage, location, who knows what, relationship status *before*, observable behavior, consequences, what persists, unearned resolution to avoid, and later return-journey callback if earned.

Do not lock who travels with whom at every minute until scene sequence and playable progression are reconciled against the actual local development files. Never position all four together in opening material when the story requires early separation.
