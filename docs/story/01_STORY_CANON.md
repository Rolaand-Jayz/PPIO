# Story canon — approved foundation

## Premise and thesis
The protagonists are living toys in a specific family home, at a scale where normal domestic surroundings become a journey. The story follows innocence giving way to knowledge, responsibility, courage, family sacrifice, trust and meaningful choice. It does **not** equate maturity with misery or innocence with stupidity. Cover statement: *“The things we learn through play become the principles by which we live.”*

## People and party
- CJ: twelve years old.
- Twins: newborn at the relevant ending; do **not** import the old SHMUP's four-year-old twins.
- Toys: Jayze, Kip, Rallo, Captain Crusher, later personally known as Jonnie.
- Jonnie's personal name is a later earned revelation through growing intimacy, not opening-scene exposition.
- Initial apparent wins against ordinary threats are easy. The Unknown's arrival breaks the toys' confidence and forces separation.
- Early playable emphasis is two protagonists, with all four becoming playable during the journey. Exact initial staging must reconcile with the current local source before being declared locked.

## Geographic and emotional route
The journey proceeds outward through the family home toward the backyard and alley, seeking and learning from family belongings. Rescues/encounters include the father's possessions near the long hallway, the mother's near the kitchen before the outside journey, and grandparents' heirlooms in the alley; exact staging and abilities are not yet fully locked. Having discovered the alley promises more unknown rather than escape, the toys choose to return and protect their family, now knowing that safety and victory are not guaranteed.

The return journey revisits places, people, decisions and vulnerabilities with changed understanding. Physical geography must stay consistent. Do not invent connecting doors or rooms merely to enable a beat.

## Character growth payoffs
- Jayze learns humility and empathy, later notices a formerly overlooked toy and defers to Kip when appropriate.
- Kip earns real autonomy and demonstrates competence leading rather than remaining the sidekick.
- Rallo faces fear and disorientation without Kip and chooses courage; on the return he can be the steadier presence.
- Jonnie's isolation and independence are pressured by abandonment and perceived betrayal; she ultimately accepts help, forgiveness and trust.
- Jonnie/Jayze and Kip/Rallo each have distinct relational challenges and earned return payoffs.

## CJ rule
CJ's own experience parallels the toys' emotional arc, but the game may show **only what the toys could see or hear from their actual positions**. No omniscient CJ cutaways outside their visibility. Use environmental evidence, overheard speech, glimpses and later reinterpretation instead.

## Resolution
The toys protect their family without knowing they will be inherited. Their hard-earned victory is briefly bittersweet; the responsibility of protecting the newborn twins becomes a reward and a new chapter of care. Credits may use still images in a distinctly different cinematic art treatment. Preserve the emotional movement toward earned responsibility rather than a generic horror or nihilistic twist.

## Protected unknowns
Do not invent the Unknown's final nature or reveal it as a company, ordinary household object or simulation; visual and behavioral specifications remain unapproved. Do not assume specific heirloom appearance/powers, house dimensions, toy sizes or a revised chronology. Proxies remain noncanonical.
